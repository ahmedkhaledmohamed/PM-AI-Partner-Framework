# Framework purpose and usefulness pilot

## Problem Framing

The framework's engineering checks did not establish whether other product managers benefit from it. Its purpose needed to reflect its strongest contribution: a practical method for turning product questions and evidence into reviewable decisions.

## Approach

Clarified the intended audience and the boundary between the guide, reusable skills and private workspaces. Prepared an observed three-participant pilot covering task completion, author assistance, output corrections and voluntary repeat use before further feature investment.

## Key Decisions

Keep initialization optional and pause feature expansion while gathering evidence. Treat existing tooling proposals separately, and keep participant research private unless an aggregate summary is separately approved for sharing.

## Outcome

Prepared documentation only; no participants have been recruited and no effectiveness results exist. Changed-document links and whitespace checks passed; existing upstream validation failures remain covered by a separate open proposal. The contribution extractor found no matching sessions, so this record summarizes the visible task.
