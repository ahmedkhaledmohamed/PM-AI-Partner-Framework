# Clarify team scope
Start at the user's current stage; do not impose a four-week onboarding schedule.
Absorb: compare the charter, handover, product strategy, stakeholder expectations, and current work. Record conflicting accounts instead of averaging them into consensus.
Audit: distinguish implemented capability, operational ownership, stakeholder expectation, and desired future responsibility. Verify code and delivery evidence where claims depend on them.
Articulate: describe the team's purpose, users, value, success measures, and boundaries with adjacent teams. Name the handoff interfaces and unresolved ownership questions.
Align: prepare a reviewable proposal and questions for relevant owners. Label proposed ownership and unconfirmed agreement; do not assign colleagues or communicate externally without authorization.
Use the output the user needs: a brief boundary explanation, capability table, charter, or decision memo. Include source dates and confidence where uncertainty affects the conclusion.
Treat planning-cycle work as instances of enduring product responsibilities, not a replacement for them.
