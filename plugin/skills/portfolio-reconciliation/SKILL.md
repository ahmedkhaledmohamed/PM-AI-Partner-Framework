# Reconcile a work inventory
Define the organization, planning periods, object types, statuses, and question. Read current upstream maintained records and use available organization-approved planning workflows for canonical planning semantics and writes.
Record filters, pagination completion, retrieval dates, source revisions, exclusions, and inaccessible historical coverage. Retrieve all pages within scope; selected examples are not an inventory.
Preserve stable source IDs and many-to-many relationships. Deduplicate records without collapsing distinct deliverables; keep unclassified and unmatched work visible.
Keep recurring product themes and their success measures separate from cycle-specific work. Preserve original cycle history and distinguish ideas, proposed rollover, confirmed scope, and measured delivery.
Separate Epic tracking status from delivery status. Explicitly documented BAU or tracking elsewhere is not cancellation; cancellation alone does not prove BAU or completion. Preserve unknown reasons.
A cancelled project does not cancel a shared capability gap. Check alternate delivery homes and related but distinct outcomes, such as data retrieval versus state synchronization.
Separate additional-org tagging, requested contribution, and confirmed commitment. Do not assign owners or commitments from tags alone.
Reconcile counts and discrepancies against source totals. Show a compact inventory summary plus unresolved coverage and changes; write narratives in direct product language.
Do not mutate planning systems, trackers, classification decisions, or curated sheet order during a read-only reconciliation.

Use meaningful titles beside IDs and verify record identities after user sorting or grouping. Reuse one underlying deliverable across multiple contribution homes. Link original and proposed job definitions so a reviewer can accept some changes within a request. Clearly distinguish confirmed continuation from a possible rollover; preserve cancellations specific to the delivery home.
