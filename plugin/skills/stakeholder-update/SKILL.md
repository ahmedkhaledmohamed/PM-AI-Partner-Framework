# Draft a stakeholder update
Establish the period, audience, and sources. Use current upstream records for mutable repository status. Label stale snapshots and unavailable sources.
Lead with the material change or decision. Report outcomes and reader impact; include activity only when it explains progress or a blocker.
For a full report, a useful default is summary, status, progress, key metrics, risks, asks, and next period. A short chat update can be a few sentences. Do not pad missing sections with repeated TBD headings.
Keep tracking status, delivered functionality, rollout, and measured impact distinct. A closed ticket does not establish launch; a shipped change does not establish impact.
For risks, give the trigger or evidence, consequence, mitigation, and decision needed. Do not invent owners or imply agreement.
Preserve metric definitions and comparison windows. Clearly label targets and forecasts. Revisit the narrative when corrected evidence changes it.
End with a specific ask only when one exists. Drafting is not permission to send a message or update a tracker.
