## Shared investigations

For shared research or an unfocused startup in this investigation workspace, read INVESTIGATIONS.md and the installed shared-investigations skill. On an unfocused startup, list open investigations with IDs, coordinators and questions before asking the contributor to choose. Use `investigations/registry.json` to discover open work or open the supplied ID directly. Read only the selected record and relevant sources. Do not interrupt unrelated work with investigation onboarding.

Before contributing, check current shared context and open proposals through authorized tools. Accept only contributor-selected local inputs. Existing reports, data and charts keep their maintained homes; add references rather than another evidence bundle. Preserve attribution, uncertainty and disagreements. No new findings means no invented contribution. Apply local review and publication policy; a coordinator accepts reconciled conclusions.
